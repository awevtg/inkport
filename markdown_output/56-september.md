# 5–6 september

*By samayra — Sun, 06 Sep 2026 18:19:05 GMT*

**Tags:** `diary-writing`, `nonfiction`, `this-happened-to-me`, `journaling`, `creative-writing`

this is not january 19, and not 2024. it is not a diary entry that i thought i would write here. except my wishes never come true, similar to the typo i made here. is it called that even when the person is writing this manually? i don’t think that’s a word. my writing is becoming more inspired by, or inclined towards, technical words. for a person who thought she could keep her thoughts and her work in different spectrums, witnessing those colours merge is giving me no joy, though. as if finally, all my theories, philosophies, and aspirations are waking up, calling me out to wake up — or just to stop daydreaming. this is not the only side of my life where i have confronted myself with being wrong. there is no surprise when these things turn out to be what they actually are. how many times would a girl wish upon a breaking, falling star? you are hopeless, free-falling under a planet you know nothing about. realizing this doesn’t make me stop wishing again, though. but this time, i wish to aspire a little less. as if it might come true. i am finally sick. sick as i always was. no one wants to talk about their weakness, but i am not talking, am i? i am writing it here. nobody will read this again. i don’t think i will. this diary is old, and even though the metal binding makes it look regal, it is difficult to write here. i was always sick. the last time was today in the morning, when i got a fever. feverish enough to feel the shivers run through my body. my face planted on the pillow, which i hated the day i came here. closed my ears, which were feeling heavy despite removing my earrings a few weeks earlier. hollow. empty. hearing the white noise of the fan cutting the air into slices and shapes. i am listening to a youtube playlist of an hour-long timeline to avoid ads. the channel has a few hundred subscribers, with some thousand views here and there. i always wondered who these people were. how they thought of opening a channel just like that. recently, i watched *sterling point*, where, after a one-night stand, she says she might be “going on a spree or a crisis, doing things she never thought she could,” stating that someone should stop her when she opens a youtube channel. i gave a hollow laugh, advising my thoughts to approve of her. to realize that some things are cool only to some people. days here are not linear. i remember eating bread two days ago and calling it a thursday. i open a book i finished a few days ago, realizing it’s already over. she is already in the hospital, delivering her daughter, who is just 7 lbs. i thought about writing about the book i read — writing as in publishing it online — but my lack of proof, of feeling like an imposter claiming to be a writer or a “blogger,” has limited me to an already-lacking undercurrent in the river where i swam occasionally, seldomly making myself new again. there is no definite timeline. i don’t see the way i will ever be back there. even writing here, i am already analyzing, convincing myself that if i see something good, something that matches my statue of marble on the internet, made of just 22 rules, maybe then i will feel that it would be something. my mother said that when i was sick, when i was earliest, i was born sick. kept under the blue light, which i no longer recall, kept there for a week with kids like me. born too early here. unhappy and sick. it is the fifth of september, 2026, when i am writing this. my fever is down now, just a few degrees below what it was, but normal. my mother is not here to count 60. my father is not here, clicking his fingers on the car key. i see the timestamp in numbers cross 61 seconds on the screen, closing my eyes and seeing 99 on the digital screen. i think i can write more, but i don’t want to. just like i know i can make this life worthwhile, but don’t have any desire to. there is everything i could do in this world. the world is of endless possibilities. someone is out there solving data-storing methods in bacterial DNA, someone is in her bed, listening to Gracie Abrams with the shared album of her roommate, learning ADME processes because her playlists are too depressing. my fever is almost gone, cough is still here, knocking my head against a wall isn’t working anymore, sneezing is, well, the meds work. that’s all i could say. the results came, my immune system, which was a great topic of discussion from yesterday, was not good. i already knew this, but seeing it again wasn’t a good sight, seeing it way under the normal range, highlighted in bold on my screen. hospitals are weird, seeing parents drag their crying toddlers with bags and sippers, witnessing older parents being dragged around by their offspring in wheelchairs, waiting for elevators, recognizing every stop where my hands or clothes touched. sitting on my chair, tired from the ride, cleaning my desk and chair with wet wipes. in the first blog, i said i would continue this till i turn a year older. i didn’t give much context to the blog thing and why i am writing it. i am turning a year older this coming week, which is just a few days away. i remember when i was 14, i scheduled an email to myself for when i would be 43. i don’t know why that age. i thought at that time, maybe this will be the age when i would do everything and would obviously forget what a 14-year-old me ever was, including her whimsical dreams and rock-band obsessions. somewhere, along the way of writing this, i remember asking my mother about her teenage years. she remembers, i guess, in bits, and maybe that’s why i chose this. i don’t remember her. nor do i want to open the email in my drafts to uncover the suspense. so, why am i writing this? just a way i would remember. remember writing that i forget, that even i forget. there is somewhere else, a place i can visit to just remember.
