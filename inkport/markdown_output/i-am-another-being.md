# I am another being.

*By samayra — Fri, 24 Jul 2026 13:10:21 GMT*

**Tags:** `creative-writing`, `tag1`, `writing-life`, `personal-essay`, `tag2`

I am another being. I am no longer what I used to be. I am no one and everyone. I am just another human being.

For reasons that are unknown to you and known only to me, I have arrived at a place in my life where I am no one. I am not the girl I used to be, nor will I be the woman I once imagined. When I write this, I realise how many circles I have just looped in my head to avoid telling you the truth. And in these moments I realise that some parts of my past are still with me. The parts where I wrote to tell everyone how I have become a liar and how I don’t want to write all my truths anywhere.

When I posted that 2024 essay, it was four pages long and handwritten, belonging to my old journal, which I am taking with me. Documenting it made it almost a thousand word essay. I posted it while daydreaming about a day when I will exclaim about my past self being too honest or ridicule myself for being an anonymous person taking pride in sharing something which was part of something personal.

Maybe someday I will finally feel something close to this feeling, or someday later I will feel completely opposite. I am the one who likes the future much more than the present. This future does not involve five years of paying taxes, seven years of having a dog to care for, or ten years of trying to open a publishing house.

This future is just ten to fifteen days away, I tell you. It is also a future where I know, unlike the rest of the futures I often daydream about, what I will be wearing; where I will be staying, which metro I will be taking, how my glasses will fog up coming out of the air conditioner chamber, and how I will sit and listen and talk to myself about accepting it all, accepting everything I will take myself up to, and accepting that this is what I am becoming, just another being.
