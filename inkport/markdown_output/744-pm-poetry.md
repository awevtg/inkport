JULY 2026

I don’t know who I am without them. The clock clicks a moment. It’s one a.m., and I imagine myself calling them back. I see the smile, the tired eyes, the swollen heart. My barren land, my cloudless, clear sky. Rain in my sky, rain I collect in empty bottles. The weaver of gold, the preacher of my life — you are old, and I am not young.
