# Am i still in july?

*By samayra — Mon, 27 Jul 2026 03:44:49 GMT*

**Tags:** `writing`, `memoir`, `self-reflection`, `journaling`, `creative-non-fiction`

24/07/2026

It is July, and Jennie’s new song, A Little Less Than a Lover, is on loop in my ears. I have been procrastinating since last week, avoiding some important articles while cramming maths like a human I no longer recognise. As I write this, I realise that the last time I wrote in this document was in June, and now July is coming to an end. Before I was going to write this, I wondered why some months have 30 and 31 days, like why August has 31 but not September. It explained that the reason is an amalgam of superstitions of Romans and their imperial vanity. Julius Caesar (the month of July was named after him) gave July thirty-one days because it was named after him, and Augustus, the jealous fellow, wanted his month, August, to have thirty-one days too.

Writing is hard these days. Not as liberating as it once was. Life keeps interrupting the sentence. I have become way too curious about real life, and I have no exams for the time being, yet no words seem to come through.

So, my writing regimen has reduced to a bliss I fathomed in a fortnight, but this time, it was a month later. The world has a lot going on too; the world has finally become a participating factor in what I do, maybe in one way instead of the backdrop I barely glance at, continuing my act. Perhaps I am allowing myself to feel things I once ignored.

I have been taking decisions; I am afraid of making decisions.

When my sister was here last month, I asked her how you can just do things. She looked at me funny, but I couldn’t explain more. I am afraid of making wrong decisions. I am afraid of finding truths that I no longer see, finding lies I will never uncover and learning more about myself in these situations where I end up hating myself instead of loving.

What if I am not normal? What if the shell I lived in until now was a better place? What should I not do? What do you think will happen to me? How do you just live? Is it scary? Is it normal to have so many questions? I think I am scared; I realise this again and again while writing this. Maybe she did it too, taking me in a hug.

I miss you.

25/07/2026

It is a Saturday, and after months I am waiting for a Sunday the way I used to. In the school where I spent most of my primary education, the classes were only held until midday. I only liked that day mainly because it was activity day and every period extended a little more than usual, longer and less tiring.

I always find myself in these situations where I want to continue just a little more; I don’t want it to end, but even if it were definite, just not now. Maybe the song I am listening to shouldn’t linger after playing but just play for a few seconds more. In these seconds maybe I will cherish it more and miss it somewhat less.

Later at school, I further transitioned, making Saturdays the same as another weekday. On those Saturdays, I missed myself, who was in third grade, finger painting on a white sheet with her classmates, then standing on the wooden table, pinning up the chart paper on the maroon velvet cushion board and, with paint still left on her hands, chasing her friends in blue hallways and white stairs, feeling sick because of the smell of vinegar and baking soda on school exhibition projects.

I know when I changed; I think that it was my misery. These memories never arrive in waves; the waves in the years until now I learned or conditioned myself to surf.

I still remember the sea, how I slipped my unlucky foot, just twelve, opening my eyes finally, seeing nothing and everything with my eyes burning, licking my lips, tasting salt, and how I was lifted above by my armpits, told it was time to go back and that I could come back tomorrow. I haven’t been to the sea since that day.

My tomorrow never came.
